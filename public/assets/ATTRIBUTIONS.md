# Asset Attributions

Every asset used in STARWAKE is listed here with its source, author, and license.
**Rule:** No asset may be added to the project without a corresponding entry in this file, in the same commit.

CC0 assets require no attribution. CC-BY assets appear in the in-game Credits scene.
